Why direct

Routing a file through a server means it sits somewhere before it reaches your recipient. Wireline skips that step.

End-to-end by construction

WebRTC opens an encrypted channel straight between the two browsers. Only the initial handshake passes through a coordination server — never your files.

No artificial size limit

Since nothing uploads to a server, there's no plan tier capping you at 2GB. Practical limits come from your connection and device memory, not from us.

Nothing left behind

Close the tab and the connection is gone. No history, no server copy, nothing to delete later because there was never a copy made.
